Quick answers

Good to know.

Here are simple answers to common questions before you contact us.

I'm not sure what or where to study. Can I still contact BCAS?

Yes. You do not need to have everything decided. Share your interests, qualifications and goals, and ask what options may fit you.

What should I prepare before asking about a course?

If convenient, have your latest education results, preferred subject, possible destination and rough start date ready. Do not send passwords, bank details or passport information through social media.

Is the online application form on this concept website live?

No. This website is a front-end concept. To make an official enquiry, contact BCAS directly through the phone/WhatsApp links above or visit the official website.

Where can I check visa requirements?

Visa rules vary by destination and can change. Use official government immigration websites and ask an adviser what to verify for your situation. BCAS cannot guarantee a visa decision.
